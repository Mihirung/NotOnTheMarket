Quiet Market
Quiet Market: the homes that aren't for sale. Yet.
Prototype v5 for internal review. No postcards are printed or posted from this page. Every postcode area in England and Wales. Views are computed house by house for every address point in the country: one viewpoint at the upper windows and one in the garden, from Environment Agency and Natural Resources Wales 1 m LIDAR, OS OpenMap Local buildings, OS Open UPRN and Copernicus terrain. Each address joins its sale records through its exact postcode (ONS UPRN Directory), so counts of homes with a given view are estimates. Likelihood figures come from a real model trained on HM Land Registry Price Paid Data (England & Wales, 1995–2019, 24.3M transactions; out-of-time AUC ). Air quality and health-access figures are real, from AHAH v4 inputs. Drive times are approximated from straight-line distance, not routed. Offer figures use a proof-of-concept pricing rule, an assumption until campaign data exists.
