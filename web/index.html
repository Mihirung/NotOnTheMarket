Quiet Market
Quiet Market — the homes that aren't for sale. Yet.
Prototype v2 for internal review. No postcards are printed or posted from this page. Likelihood figures come from a real model trained on HM Land Registry Price Paid Data (England & Wales, 1995–2019, 24.3M transactions; out-of-time AUC 0.588). Air quality and health-access figures are real, from AHAH v4 inputs. Drive times are approximated from straight-line distance at 50 km/h, not routed. Offer figures use a proof-of-concept pricing rule — an assumption until campaign data exists. Demo area: Exeter.
