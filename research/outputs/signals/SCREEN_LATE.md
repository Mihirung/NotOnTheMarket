# Screen: do energy certificates help find homes not yet on the market?

Homes are scored on 1 January. A later seller completes between July of that year and June of the next,
so was, with few exceptions, not yet listed on the scoring day. Figures are later sellers found in the best
tenth of each postcode district, times random, test years 2017 and 2018. The gain is the second step with
certificates against the same second step without them. Ranges are 95% and cover sampling only.

## Owners: all

| Ranking | Later sellers found | Share of list that sells later | Share of list already under way |
|---|---|---|---|
| deployed model | 1.662 | 6.5% | 3.3% |
| deployed model refitted with certificates | 1.621 | 6.3% | 4.5% |
| second step aimed at later sales, no certificates | 1.655 | 6.4% | 3.1% |
| second step aimed at later sales, with certificates | 1.701 | 6.6% | 3.9% |

Gain from certificates: +0.045 (+0.029 to +0.065).
