# What refreshing the scores every month would add

A test, not a change to the product. The product scores homes once a year, on 1 January. Here the ranking is looked at on the
first of every month: homes sold since 1 January have left it, and the target is a sale completing in the twelve months that
follow. The years are 2017 and 2018, the homes are the test chunks', and each is scored by notice steps fitted on other
homes in the other year. The first step keeps its January figure throughout, so this is what the notices alone gain from
being read sooner. Figures are sellers found in the best tenth of each postcode district, times random.

## Reading it

Averaged over the year, the notices add +0.010 to the best tenth when the product is scored once a year and +0.023 when it is
refreshed every month: 2.3 times as much. A notice says most in its first six months, and a yearly score does not
hold the ones published since January.

## Weekly or monthly

Reading the notices a month late costs +0.0030 (+0.0026 to +0.0034) on average (the last column of the first table). A ranking
refreshed monthly is on average half a month behind and one refreshed weekly half a week, so a weekly refresh is worth about
+0.0012 (+0.0010 to +0.0013) more than a monthly one; for homes owned 20 years or more, +0.0037 (+0.0026 to +0.0048).
The Land Registry adds sales once a month, so only the notices can be read weekly.
