# Homes named in a notice that the sale records do not hold

The model scores only homes with a sale on record since 1995. These are the others: homes named in a notice with no
sale on record when the year began, 2017 and 2018 together. A home with no notice and no sale on record
cannot be measured the same way; homes that do have a record sell at 4.0% a year. The product counts a
home in only where the whole range is above that.

| Notice | Published | Homes | First sold in the year (95% range) | Completes July to June |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bankruptcy order | 1 to 6 months before | 7,327 | 1.1% (0.9 to 1.4) | 1.3% |
| Bankruptcy order | 7 to 12 months before | 7,230 | 1.3% (1.0 to 1.5) | 1.3% |
| Bankruptcy order | 13 to 24 months before | 14,726 | 1.5% (1.3 to 1.7) | 1.6% |
| Bankruptcy order | 25 to 36 months before | 16,462 | 1.5% (1.3 to 1.7) | 1.5% |
| Bankruptcy order | 37 to 48 months before | 19,463 | 1.4% (1.2 to 1.6) | 1.4% |

Lower bounds: an address that fails to match its later sale record counts as not sold.
