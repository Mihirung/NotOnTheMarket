# Bankruptcy orders: do the homes sell?

A sample of 632 notices from The Gazette. 608 are for England and Wales with an address; 280 (46%) match a home with a sale record, and 266 of those were last bought before the bankruptcy order.

| Year scored | Homes still in the same hands | Sold in that year (95% range) | Homes owned as long sell at | Times |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 261 | 5.4% (3.1 to 8.4) | 4.3% | 1.2 |
| The year after that | 247 | 6.5% (3.6 to 9.7) | 4.2% | 1.6 |

Sold within so many months of the bankruptcy order: 6 months 0%, 12 months 2%, 18 months 6%, 24 months 8%, 36 months 17%.
The notice appears a median of 0 months after the bankruptcy order.

## Homes with no sale on record before

342 notices are for homes with no sale since 1995 before the bankruptcy order. The model does not cover these homes: the sale records hold a home only once it has been sold.

| Year | Homes not yet sold | First sold in that year (95% range) |
|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 342 | 1.2% (0.3 to 2.3) |
| The year after that | 338 | 0.9% (0.0 to 2.1) |

Sold by the end of 2019 (notices to 2016): 4%. Lower bounds: an address that fails to match counts as not sold.

## From the month the notice appears

The product can act on a notice when it is published, so this is its own clock. All homes sell at about 4.4% a year.

| Homes | Not yet sold when the notice appears | Sold within 12 months (95% range) | Sold within 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homes the model covers (bought before) | 266 | 3.8% (1.5 to 6.0) | 10.2% (6.8 to 13.9) |
| Homes with no sale on record before | 342 | 0.6% (0.0 to 1.5) | 1.5% (0.3 to 2.9) |

Scale: about 13,704 notices a year point to about 321 sellers in the following year, 0.1% of all sellers among homes with a sale record.
