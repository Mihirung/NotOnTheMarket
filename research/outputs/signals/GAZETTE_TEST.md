# Deceased-estate notices: do the homes sell?

A sample of 714 notices from The Gazette. 560 are for England and Wales with an address; 277 (50%) match a home with a sale record, and 97 of those were last bought before the death.

| Year scored | Homes still in the same hands | Sold in that year (95% range) | Homes owned as long sell at | Times |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 66 | 28.8% (18.2 to 39.4) | 4.0% | 7.1 |
| The year after that | 47 | 12.8% (4.3 to 23.4) | 3.8% | 3.4 |

Sold within so many months of the death: 6 months 9%, 12 months 36%, 18 months 52%, 24 months 56%, 36 months 58%.
The notice appears a median of 3 months after the death.

## Homes with no sale on record before

463 notices are for homes with no sale since 1995 before the death. The model does not cover these homes: the sale records hold a home only once it has been sold.

| Year | Homes not yet sold | First sold in that year (95% range) |
|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 350 | 14.6% (10.9 to 18.3) |
| The year after that | 299 | 4.0% (2.0 to 6.4) |

Sold by the end of 2019 (notices to 2016): 39%. Lower bounds: an address that fails to match counts as not sold.

## From the month the notice appears

The product can act on a notice when it is published, so this is its own clock. All homes sell at about 4.4% a year.

| Homes | Not yet sold when the notice appears | Sold within 12 months (95% range) | Sold within 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homes the model covers (bought before) | 97 | 43.3% (33.0 to 52.6) | 53.6% (43.3 to 62.9) |
| Homes with no sale on record before | 450 | 28.4% (24.4 to 32.9) | 34.4% (30.0 to 38.9) |

Scale: about 35,757 notices a year point to about 1,783 sellers in the following year, 0.3% of all sellers among homes with a sale record.
