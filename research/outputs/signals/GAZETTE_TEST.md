# Deceased-estate notices: do the homes sell?

A sample of 205 notices from The Gazette. 169 are for England and Wales with an address; 82 (48%) match a home with a sale record, and 27 of those were last bought before the death.

| Year scored | Homes still in the same hands | Sold in that year (95% range) | Homes owned as long sell at | Times |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 14 | 21.4% (0.0 to 42.9) | 3.9% | 5.4 |
| The year after that | 11 | 18.2% (0.0 to 45.5) | 4.0% | 4.6 |

Sold within so many months of the death: 6 months 7%, 12 months 48%, 18 months 56%, 24 months 59%, 36 months 59%.
The notice appears a median of 3 months after the death.

## Homes with no sale on record before

142 notices are for homes with no sale since 1995 before the death. The model does not cover these homes: the sale records hold a home only once it has been sold.

| Year | Homes not yet sold | First sold in that year (95% range) |
|---|---|---|
| The calendar year after the notice | 99 | 8.1% (3.0 to 14.1) |
| The year after that | 91 | 3.3% (0.0 to 7.7) |

Sold by the end of 2019 (notices to 2016): 39%. Lower bounds: an address that fails to match counts as not sold.

## From the month the notice appears

The product can act on a notice when it is published, so this is its own clock. All homes sell at about 4.4% a year.

| Homes | Not yet sold when the notice appears | Sold within 12 months (95% range) | Sold within 24 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homes the model covers (bought before) | 27 | 48.1% (29.6 to 66.7) | 59.3% (40.7 to 77.8) |
| Homes with no sale on record before | 138 | 28.3% (21.0 to 35.5) | 34.1% (26.1 to 42.0) |

Scale: about 35,757 notices a year point to about 1,224 sellers in the following year, 0.2% of all sellers among homes with a sale record.
